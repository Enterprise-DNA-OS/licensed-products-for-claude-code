# Selection and source record

Checked 15 November 2026. New family: consumer-product-licensing. This covers merchandise licence terms and royalty sales, rather than music royalty allocation or marketing asset permissions. SourceDay's published Supplier Deliveries rebuild was the implementation reference.

FADEL's [FY2025 company results](https://www.lse.co.uk/rns/results-for-the-year-ended-31-december-2025-aog5rxp42asf5f1.html?mobile_view=desktop) report LicenSee annual contract values of US$10,000 to US$50,000, with implementation separate. This is a company-reported range, not a public price plan, a typical NZ/AU invoice or a savings estimate. The [vendor product page](https://fadel.com/licensee/) has a demo enquiry and no numeric subscription price. The comparison page therefore uses no numeric vendor price.

Scores, 1 to 5: annual bill 2, data-shaped 5, our buyers 2, search demand 2, one-session buildable 4, import path 3. Total 18/30. The bill score uses the lower end of the published range. Buyer fit is an inference for manufacturers and importers selling licensed consumer goods. Local customer and CRM-context text searches found no confirmed LicenSee subscriber or invoice. No live CRM read was performed. Alternative-search results establish an existing topic, not measured search volume.

Compared Rightsline: 1,5,1,2,4,3 = 16/30. Its [comparison page](https://www.rightsline.com/comparison/) describes configurable pricing but no verified five-figure bill was found. Compared Valutico: 1,3,2,3,2,4 = 15/30. No verified five-figure annual bill was found, and its licensed market-data component is not a suitable database-only replacement.

The [LicenSee datasheet](https://fadel.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/LicenSee-Datasheet.pdf) describes agreement terms, spreadsheet sales intake, royalty review and formatted statements. It does not establish export column names. The importer uses an explicit mapping from the source sales workbook. Vendor-specific reports and more complex contract rules are custom work.

This implementation has no FADEL code, assets or contract templates. It provides flat-rate royalty working records, not every feature of LicenSee. Claims about our own checks are covered by the smoke tests. The ten questions are demonstrated queries; no claim is made that FADEL cannot answer them.
